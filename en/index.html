nunc.

Latin for now. One of the questions this is about:

What is a person worth when their work costs nothing?

A publisher is a filter. From now on, we do the filtering.

Since 2002, gannaca has written down what is coming for companies and society, dated and sourced. Some of it became books with other publishers, some became keynotes. A large part stayed in the drawer: too specific for a catalogue, too early for the market, too uncomfortable for an editor who plays it safe.

NUNC takes what stayed in the drawer and brings it out when it is ready. Not when an autumn list has a slot. Whatever passes the filter has to touch at least one of the questions we are having to ask ourselves afresh. Add to that what our network has to say: 1,500 decision-makers in 55 countries, researchers, founders, artists, many of whom have written something that fits no publisher's grid.

  • fastWeeks pass between the final proof and the order button. We do not run spring or autumn lists.
  • simpleOne contact, short paths, no committees. If you publish with us, you talk to people who publish themselves.
  • beautifulDesign is part of the argument here, never a line item. Every title gets a designer with a name.

The questions the daily grind crowds out.

People who carry responsibility rarely have time for them. That is exactly why they are this publisher's programme: every title, every note, every guest essay answers at least one of them. If you want to know why a question matters right now, open it. One sentence, one source, nothing more.

Who owns the future when three companies own the models?

The compute behind the largest AI models has grown four- to fivefold per year for years, and only a handful of companies can still pay for it. Source: Epoch AI, Trends

What is a person worth when their work costs nothing?

AI systems match human performance on more benchmarks every year while the cost per query falls by orders of magnitude. Source: Stanford AI Index

Who is liable when the machine decides?

Since August 2026 the EU's obligations for high-risk AI systems apply, and "the system suggested it" no longer counts as an excuse. Source: EU AI Act, timeline

Which truth holds when every fake is perfect?

Trust in news has hovered around 40 percent worldwide for years, and the worry about telling real from fake grows with every survey. Source: Reuters Institute, Digital News Report

What are we measured by when nobody checks anymore?

The Global Risks Report lists mis- and disinformation as the world's top short-term risk. Source: World Economic Forum, Global Risks Report

How much future can we still afford, and who pays for it?

At today's emissions the remaining carbon budget for 1.5 degrees is used up within a few years. Source: Global Carbon Budget

What do we owe the people living in 2080?

The decisions of this decade lock in climate consequences for centuries, in either direction. Source: IPCC, Sixth Assessment Report

Is stability still the norm, or already the exception?

At least six of nine planetary boundaries have been crossed; the system our institutions were built for no longer exists in that form. Source: Stockholm Resilience Centre, Planetary Boundaries

Is democracy an operating system that needs updates, or one we are uninstalling?

Global freedom has declined year after year for almost two decades. Source: Freedom House, Freedom in the World

What is Europe when it no longer writes the rules but merely follows them?

In the last 50 years not a single company worth more than 100 billion euros today has been founded from scratch in the EU. Source: Draghi report on EU competitiveness

Who holds a country together that grows older than any country before it?

The number of people aged 67 and over in Germany rises by at least four million by the mid-2030s while the working-age population shrinks. Source: Federal Statistical Office, population projection

How many people are you planning for: today's, or those of 2050?

World population keeps growing into the 2080s to about 10.3 billion and then declines; Germany declines earlier. Source: UN World Population Prospects

Who inherits, and what will they do with it?

Up to 400 billion euros change hands in Germany every year through inheritance and gifts, more than ever before. Source: DIW Weekly Report

Who decides when nobody has time to think?

Only about one in five employees worldwide feels engaged at work; decisions are made in organisations that are inwardly absent. Source: Gallup, State of the Global Workplace

Why are we so connected and so alone?

The WHO set up its own Commission on Social Connection because loneliness now counts as a health risk on the scale of smoking. Source: WHO Commission on Social Connection

What if German Angst is just a habit?

Since 2002, 139 of 330 dated foresight calls have publicly come true; the future is not fate, it is bookkeeping. Source: gannaca foresight tracker
