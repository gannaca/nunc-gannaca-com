Latin for now. In Cologne it is just about now. This page was typeset on 3 September 2026.

A publisher is a filter. From now on, we do the filtering.

Since 2002, gannaca has written down what is coming for companies and society, dated and sourced. Some of it became books with other publishers, some became keynotes. A large part stayed in the drawer: too specific for a catalogue, too early for the market, too uncomfortable for an editor who plays it safe.

NUNC takes what stayed in the drawer and brings it out when it is ready. Not when an autumn list has a slot. Add to that what our network has to say: 1,500 decision-makers in 55 countries, researchers, founders, artists, many of whom have written something that fits no publisher's grid.

  • fastWeeks pass between the final proof and the order button. We do not run spring or autumn lists.
  • simpleOne contact, short paths, no committees. If you publish with us, you talk to people who publish themselves.
  • beautifulDesign is part of the argument here, never a line item. Every title gets a designer with a name.

DEUTSCHMUT. The new hard currency.

A flip book about the mood of courage, written by Christopher Peterka and designed by Dirk Kels. 86 pages cut into three independently turning strips yield 42,875 grammatically correct sentences about a Germany that no longer explains itself through German Angst. The Collector's Edition is limited to 100 numbered, hand-signed copies. German language.
